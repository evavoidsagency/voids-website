About

WE FILL THE VOIDS.

The job market is full of gaps. Students with talent companies never see. Companies with opportunities students never come across. Those empty spaces, those voids, we fill by connecting the right people to the right place.

We look beyond the CV: at motivation, working style and ambition. Personal, selective and on a mission to give everyone a fairer start.

Our edge

WE GET TO KNOW YOUNG TALENT PERSONALLY.

We don't wait for CVs to come in. Through partnerships with student associations and other student organisations, and at the places where young talent meets, we get to know students in person. That way we see more than a CV shows: what drives someone, how they work, and whether that fits what an organisation is looking for.

Partners

Student associations and other student organisations: together we join forces to reach young talent.

Events

We host events for young talent ourselves and attend events run by others, to meet students in person.
