For partners

Advice for partners.

Partners can come to us for advice on attracting, hiring and retaining junior talent, from a tailored programme to in-house days. We don't offer this separately: it is part of the partnership.

Tailored advice

A programme to attract, hire and retain junior talent, tailored to your organisation.

In-house days

On set days, a VOIDS recruiter works on location with your team: coordinating directly with the managers who hire, sourcing and interviewing, without delays between two offices.

Trainings & workshops

Practical sessions for teams and managers, for example on onboarding and retaining junior talent.

Community events

We organise meet-ups like talent dinners and lunches, where companies and junior talent meet informally.

Afspraken

What we ask of you.

A partnership only works if it means something to students. That's why we set a few conditions.

€17 to €22
per hour

Paid work

Partners offer paid work experience. Working students earn €17 to €22 per hour with them. That's why the partner label means something.

A limited number of partners

We work with a limited number of partners at a time, so you stay visible and don't disappear in a long list.

Annual membership

A partnership runs per year. We discuss the price in a conversation.

Building together

Partners who join now help shape events and formats, get a more favourable rate and are first in line.
