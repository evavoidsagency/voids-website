Impact

Show that you stand for equal opportunities.

Relevant, paid work experience is not within reach for every student. As a partner you do offer it, and it shows.

What does that mean?

Paid work experience during studies, also for students who can't work without an income or a network. You make access broader.

How do you show it?

With the partner label for your website, your vacancies and your employer brand: an impact tick for your organisation.

What can you report?

An impact report on who you reach with your partnership, for your CSR and diversity reporting.

Does this count as social return?

Social return in public tenders usually targets people with a distance to the labour market. Our focus is equal opportunities for students. We'd be glad to discuss how this fits within your CSR and inclusion policy.

Frequently asked questions

What you probably want to know.

What does a partnership cost?

A partnership is an annual membership. We discuss the price in a conversation.

What does it ask of my team?

An introduction, an interview for your portrait and a short check-in each quarter. We take care of the rest.

How long does it run?

One year, in four quarters.

What if I already have a vacancy?

We'll post it for you. As a partner you post and share vacancies continuously at a more favourable rate. If you want us to find the candidate, recruitment & selection can also run continuously within your partnership.

Can anyone become a partner?

No. We work with a limited number of partners at a time and look together with you whether it fits, for example your sector, your size and whether you offer paid work experience.
