What it delivers

The reason someone wants to work with you.

Vacancies and recruitment & selection fill a position, at the moment it exists. A partnership builds the reason junior talent wants to work with you: they know you, believe your story because it comes from real people, you learn what they are looking for and you stand for something.

Being known

Students know you exist, what you do and what kind of work there is. For your next vacancy, people respond who already have an impression, instead of people seeing your name for the first time.

  • Featured in the VOIDS community
  • Vacancies included

Being believed

A vacancy text says what a company thinks of itself. A working student telling you what it's like, or an afternoon at the office, shows what it's really like.

  • A portrait of a working student or junior at your company
  • A look behind the scenes for our members Being built
  • VOIDS events Being built

Learning what junior talent is looking for

You hear what attracts students and where they drop off, and can adapt your vacancies, terms and way of working accordingly. That makes you a better employer for this group, not just a more visible one.

  • Feedback on your vacancy texts from a student's perspective
  • A student panel Being built

Standing for something

Being a partner means: an employer that offers paid work experience and widens access. That is an association you can't buy with a vacancy.

  • The partner label
  • An impact report Being built

For companies that want to be visible to junior talent they can't reach themselves, and that want to make access to work experience broader. We are still building part of the value: what isn't finished yet is marked 'being built'.

An example

How a partnership can play out.

March

A student sees the portrait of your working student.

June

The same student is at your office.

September

They apply of their own accord.

That is what a partnership delivers, and what recruitment & selection and vacancies can't: they only start once the vacancy already exists.

For partners

As a partner, you think along with us too.

Advice on attracting, hiring and retaining junior talent is part of partnerships, not separate from them. That's how we keep building your employer brand together.

Tailored advice

Thinking along on how you attract, hire and retain junior talent, tailored to your organisation.

In-house days

On set days, a VOIDS recruiter works on location with your team: syncing live with hiring managers, sourcing and interviewing, without the back-and-forth between two offices.

Trainings & workshops

Practical sessions for teams and managers, for example on onboarding and retaining junior talent.

Community events

We organise meet-ups like talent dinners and lunches, where companies and junior talent meet informally.

How it works

Clear about what we ask.

Paid work experience

Partners offer paid work experience: working students earn €17 to €22 per hour with them. That way the partner label means something to students.

A limited number of partners

We work with a few partners at a time, so you are truly visible and don't disappear in a long list.

Annual membership

An annual membership with a permanent place. We discuss the price in a conversation.

Building together

Partners help shape events and formats, get a more favourable rate and are first in line.
